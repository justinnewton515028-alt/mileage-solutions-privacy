Mileage Solutions - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Mileage Solutions ("the app") is published by Newton's Developer Solutions ("we", "us"). It is a mileage and expense tracker for freelancers and gig workers. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: your trip and expense records stay on your phone, and we do not operate any server that receives them.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the trips, expenses and vehicle names you enter. This data is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. We do not upload it, we cannot see it, and it is not shared with anyone. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, these records are deleted. Use the backup option in Settings to keep a copy.

Information we do not collect

  • The app does not use GPS or background location tracking.
  • The app does not require an account, name or email address.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Purchases and subscriptions

If you choose to buy Premium, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat, a subscription management service, to confirm whether your subscription is active. To do this, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase and subscription status from Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. For details of how these providers handle information, see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Exports and sharing

When you create a CSV, PDF or backup file, the file is saved on your device and you decide where to send it using your phone's share menu. We do not receive these files.

Tax information

Deduction figures shown in the app are estimates calculated from the information you enter and the IRS standard mileage rates. They are for record-keeping and are not tax advice. Consult a qualified tax professional.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example by adding crash reporting, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
